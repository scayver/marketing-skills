Family dentistry in Portland

Harbor Pine Dental provides family dentistry for families in Portland, including exams, cleanings, and implant consults. You can review services, meet the team, and call the front desk when you are ready to book. The practice keeps care plans in plain language so you know what happens next.

New patients can expect a written visit plan, a clear fee conversation before treatment, and reminders for the next cleaning. The team explains options for fillings, crowns, and implants without rushing the decision. Parents can book children and adults on the same morning when the schedule allows.
