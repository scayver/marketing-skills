Contact the Portland office

Call the front desk to book a cleaning, an exam, or an implant consult. The Portland office answers weekday calls and returns web requests the same day when you write before three. Bring your questions about fees, timing, and which dentist you will see.

You can also ask the team to pair a child visit with an adult visit. The office confirms the chair time before you travel. If you need to change a visit, call the same number and ask for the scheduler.
