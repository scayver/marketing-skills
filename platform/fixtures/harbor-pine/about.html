About Harbor Pine Dental

Harbor Pine Dental is a family practice in Portland. The dentists explain treatment choices in writing, show fees before you agree, and keep follow-up visits on a simple recall schedule. You can ask about cleanings, fillings, crowns, and implant consults during the first visit.

The team includes dentists, hygienists, and a front desk that answers the phone during weekday hours. Children and adults can share a morning appointment when two chairs are open. The practice does not promise a specific insurance result. It does tell you the cash fee before treatment starts.
